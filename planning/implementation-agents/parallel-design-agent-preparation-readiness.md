# Author preparation readiness

Assessed: 5 October 2026. Implements the bounded lifecycle from the
[preparation plan](parallel-design-agent-preparation-plan.md). The separate
state/treatment/assembly exploration remains deferred.

## Operating decision

Keep serial/on-demand execution as the default. Offer preparation as an explicit,
experimental option with a small forecast-driven target. Four is the maximum
managed open-author count; two remains the simultaneous authoring limit. Do not
automatically fill four slots or advertise a general speed benefit.

The current host can create, follow up, list and interrupt agents. It does not
expose confirmed thread closure or internal startup/token/cost telemetry.
Completed and retired agents therefore remain counted. The user configuration
and current reported capacity agree on sixteen spawned threads plus the primary;
the actual ceiling was not deliberately exhausted.

## Evidence boundary

The persisted lifecycle and native guarded routes have focused deterministic
coverage. Independent forward review reproduced five gaps beyond that initial
coverage: foreign-run scope, changed forecast acknowledgments, ordinary-route
author bypass, backoff clearing without greater effective capacity and accepted-work
recovery stranding a reused author. Completion
of their fixes and focused regression results is recorded in the progress ledger.

Actual role preparation finished during real UX repair and independent review.
A changed final packet rejected the old acknowledgment before native claim
authorization. A fresh coordinator context reopened the saved ledger, native
claim, exact inputs and accepted UX gate. It rejected a concurrently changed
instruction file, then resumed the same saved assignment after exact restoration,
without duplicate creation/claim or loss of accepted output.

The live pool adopted an existing UX specialist only after an actual stopped
confirmation and native verification that its completed claim could no longer
write. Two UI authors received separate exact claims under the same accepted
UX/model/foundation and the two-author limit. Detailed quality and acceptance
outcomes are recorded in the execution progress.

Both initial rendered UI reviews required the same small copy correction. Actual
same-author repair used fresh guarded claims and positive post-delivery release.
Both repaired candidates passed native validation, and independent rechecks
confirmed the intended copy changes without design regressions. Those rechecks
also found five screenshots with fallback fonts. Partial capture replacement
timed out on the prepared branch. Its final rendered whole-UI gate remains open;
do not describe that branch as independently accepted or the comparison as
quality-complete. The declared 45-minute comparison budget was not extended.

The timing comparison is inconclusive. The initial packet digests matched, but
the authors read different amounts of native implementation code. Documentation
editing and fresh-context recovery also introduced actual scheduling delay. Do
not subtract those costs to manufacture a speedup. The small trial does not prove
two independent cold startup preparations before a gate, four live authors, real
ceiling recovery, host closure or statistical performance benefit.

Once the existing UX specialist is adopted, two new fresh author contexts for a
second matched pair would exceed the four-open managed-author ceiling. Closure
is unavailable. Do not bypass that ceiling by leaving authors untracked or
relabeling specialists, and do not regenerate unchanged accepted work solely for
better timing numbers.
