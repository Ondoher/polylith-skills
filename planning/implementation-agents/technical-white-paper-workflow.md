# Linked technical white papers: working workflow

Status: first-pass implementation complete. [attach-detail](../../skills/attach-detail/SKILL.md) owns source linking, [refine-detail](../../skills/refine-detail/SKILL.md) owns research and revision, and technical context binds current paper bytes and research status.

## Source and authority

A white paper is a living, human-owned technical document. The product description declares it through `attach-detail` as `- White paper: [Title](relative/path/to/entry.md)`, pointing to the authored file at its supplied location, without imposing a standard input folder. The path is relative to the description file. The link identifies a technical issue to process; it does not make every assertion in the paper an accepted product requirement or architecture decision. The paper has one editable source. Immutable source snapshots, research reports, claim lineage, and binding records may live under `product/<product>/`, but they are support evidence, not another paper to maintain.

The product description remains authoritative for product behavior. A white paper may contain known facts, constraints, proposals, alternatives, decisions, rationale, questions, contradictions, experiments, and citations. Technical research supplies evidence, not product authority. If work on a paper reveals a new or changed product requirement, route that decision through the description and product model. Keep accepted, conditional, proposed, unresolved, and superseded technical statements distinguishable; do not silently promote a plausible answer to an accepted decision.

The product-model contract partitions every description line into source claims and classifies the labeled declaration as `reference`, with no product-record refs. `attach-detail` establishes the source syntax and verifies a live link; the reference resolver records the exact description location, target path, and target hash without importing paper prose into the PRD. It rejects ambiguous, missing, escaping, or nonlocal targets before dependent technical work. The linked paper is reparsed from its current bytes on each relevant call; a prior extraction is not a substitute for the changed source.

## Agent-assisted development

The owner and parent may develop the paper interactively from a rough draft, an existing technical note, or a focused question. A dedicated high-reasoning technical research agent receives one bounded paper and question set, the current paper, applicable product and technical decisions, repository facts, and any prior research evidence. It investigates ambiguous mechanisms and alternatives using current primary technical sources where needed, separates observed facts from inference, and returns a sourced analysis, unresolved questions, and a proposed revision or additions. The agent's output is advisory. The parent reconciles it with owner authority, system architecture, and existing decisions before applying an edit.

Consult the system architect for material runtime, trust, data, or host-boundary choices. Consult the Polylith architect when feature/service/registry ownership needs interpretation. Reuse current assessments while their inputs remain valid; neither role needs to run merely because a paper exists. Ask the owner about consequential product choices, and record technical uncertainty as a question or bounded proof when the evidence cannot settle it.

The paper has no mandatory heading sequence or fixed content template. Agents may reformat, reorganize, combine repeated points, rewrite prose, and add researched material when that improves clarity. Before editing, extract a semantic inventory of all available information: distinct claims and facts, constraints, decisions and their rationale, alternatives, open questions, uncertainties, dependencies, experiments and results, and evidence or citations. After editing, check that each prior item is still represented in the paper or is explicitly marked resolved or superseded with its reason and relevant history. Retaining information is a semantic requirement, not a verbatim-text requirement. A disproved claim remains intelligible as a superseded claim; a contradiction remains visible until resolved. Do not drop a question merely because a proposed answer was added. Record new findings with their source and confidence or decision status. An unexplained omission fails the revision check and must be restored before the edit is considered complete.

The inventory and before/after mapping are support data, not required IDs or annotations in authored prose. They make it possible to inspect whether an agent revision preserved meaning without freezing the paper's structure. The parent owns agent-applied edits and their coverage check; the research agent does not directly mutate the source or certify its own revision. Reparse owner edits as new source authority rather than automatically restoring material the owner deliberately removed.

## Publication and change impact

The general technical guide links a substantive, existing paper through an owned publication mechanism, with a short summary and status. The focused-paper page maps records backed by exact paper-source evidence to that paper. Its unresolved questions are ordinary technical `gap` records, also shown in the shared decisions and handoff pages; reuse an existing gap when the question is equivalent. The paper should link back to the general architecture and include the product and evidence summaries needed to stand alone. Do not create a dead link for an unwritten paper, or hand-edit a generated guide page to add the link.

Changing a paper invalidates its bound technical interpretation and may stale guide sections or other papers that use its decisions. Identify those consumers and reconcile shared contracts; do not silently rewrite the product model or PRD. A change in the product description, code baseline, relevant standards, or material external evidence likewise prompts a scoped recheck of affected paper claims. Preservation is evaluated against the previous authored paper and the new source, while acceptance is evaluated against current owner decisions and evidence.

## Four implementation slices

Work through these four reviewable results. `attach-detail` is already installed globally and supplies the link syntax; it does not perform any of the remaining ingestion or research work.

1. **Create the technical research agent.** Define one narrow, read-only `technical-researcher` role under `agents/`, a product-neutral role contract under `planning/implementation-agents/`, and a closed catalog entry in `governance.json`. Configure an explicit high-reasoning model and effort, then check that a fresh agent can receive one paper and bounded questions and return sourced findings, alternatives, evidence limits, open questions, and suggested revisions. It must not edit files, accept product requirements, or certify its own output. Reconcile the installer plan before making the role globally available. **Done when:** its definition loads and one synthetic research assignment produces an advisory handoff with clear evidence and status.
2. **Bind the linked source.** Extend the closed product-model/source-claim contract so the `White paper:` line is a document reference rather than a product capability or an `unclassified` claim. Resolve the relative link from the description, bind the exact target path and bytes, and make changes to either source visible to dependent technical work. Keep the paper's prose out of the PRD and product-requirement records. **Done when:** a product-neutral fixture with one linked paper produces a verified reference and a changed paper is recognized as new input.
3. **Build paper refinement.** Prefer a focused `refine-detail` orchestration skill so ordinary `refine-design` runs need not load the deeper paper workflow. On each call it reparses the current paper, gives one bounded research question to the fresh agent when evidence is needed, has the parent check material sources and reconcile specialist ownership, and applies an authored revision only after a before/after semantic inventory accounts for all prior information. Preserve source snapshots and research handoffs under `product/<product>/`; keep the paper as the sole editable source. **Done when:** a rough synthetic paper can be reorganized and expanded without losing a fact, rationale, alternative, question, or citation, and unresolved claims remain visibly unresolved.
4. **Integrate technical output.** Make technical preparation consume the verified paper binding and selected findings without treating every paper assertion as accepted architecture. Identify technical-guide sections and other papers affected by a changed shared decision. Add an owned publication link from the general guide only for an existing substantive paper; never hand-edit receipt-owned output. **Done when:** one product-repository trial produces a useful researched paper, its technical context identifies current versus stale findings, both publication links resolve, and unrelated guide content stays stable.

For the first pass, keep consultations bounded and reviewers minimal. Add positive-path tests for the new contracts; group broader failure, staleness, and interruption checks with the later hardening review after the end-to-end trial. Use a real product paper only as trial evidence in its owning repository, never as a shared default or reusable fixture.
