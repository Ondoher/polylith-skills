helper checks current identities, ownership, reference linkage, precise permitted

The treatment takes **9.24 additional minutes to initial deliveries** across
the three scopes. The sums of branch dispatch-to-guarded-acceptance intervals are
66.22 minutes baseline and 76.26 minutes treatment. Branches overlap, so these
sums are not the experiment's wall time. Neither number isolates model execution
or establishes a causal performance effect.

Meaningful candidate repair episodes are **two baseline versus six treatment**:
the baseline has two review-directed presentation batches; the treatment has three
internal repairs, one parent-directed structural repair, one independent-review
batch combining a source omission and contrast defect, and one parent-audit
readability repair. The inherited scaffold metadata correction is a separate
parent-fixture error; its actual elapsed work is retained. Forty-three final rows
in the last treatment did not prevent the source omission or an escaped rendered
quality defect. Final acceptance required repairing both.

All 64 captures were independently inspected. The final audits cover 85 frozen
per-frame obligations in each condition, plus one source relationship recorded
after drafting and applied consistently to both conditions. The original
inventories remain unchanged. The missing simultaneous host context was also
independently found by the blinded reviewer; its adverse treatment result is
retained rather than excluded because the separate relationship row was late.
One treatment receipt passed before the parent spotted unequal legibility of
present retained values. That pass, the extra audit episode and all subsequent
capture/review costs remain visible. No escaped omissions remain at final
equivalent acceptance.

The trial uses three small scopes from two unrelated synthetic source packages,
accepted UX and frozen foundations/research, with one retained author and reviewer
per condition. Two scopes share upstream UX, so they are not independent product
replications. The
baseline retains ordinary useful author checks. Reviewers do not receive condition
labels, author ledgers or other-condition output. Parent evaluation inventories
are frozen before drafting and remain separate from production checks.

One second-case reviewer received a parent source-assessment summary in a timing
reminder before its verdict. It received no oracle rows, ledger, other branch or
condition label. This is a possible judgment bias and is retained as a protocol
limitation. Retained contexts, generated draft differences and the small sample
also prevent a broad causal claim.

The baseline has zero source omissions, so a percentage omission reduction is
undefined. No omission benefit appears in two of three cases; the only nonzero
case is adverse to the treatment. Presentation repairs are counted separately
from absent/wrong source context, while their actual work and source-purpose
effects remain part of the comparison. The reported prior UX benefit was not
verified as a quantified matched comparison in the available records.

Full dispatch-to-acceptance times include coordinator/setup delays and evaluation
work. In the second pair, the baseline's captures were ready about five minutes
before review dispatch while the parent prepared the other condition. Similar
guarded times therefore do not cancel additional author work or establish a
production speedup. Raw setup/indexing failures and review-directed repairs are
retained, with source omissions classified separately from presentation and
parent-fixture errors.

## Disposition

**Do not promote from this evidence.** The author procedure can find deliberately
seeded omissions and can repair a real pending-state cue, but the matched trials
do not demonstrate reduced downstream source omissions or total repair work.
Retain the previous default and explicit opt-in assignment. No author screenshot
turn or additional aesthetic loop is added.

A narrow maintained clarification now requires source-required relationships
between host context and transient layers to be inventoried and checked in the
assembled state; separate previews are insufficient. It also reminds authors to
use existing native schema validation. These clarifications follow the observed
failures and were not applied retroactively to the measured first deliveries.
They have not yet demonstrated a comparative benefit. Any future evaluation
should freeze relational obligations before drafting and retain equivalent final
source-purpose quality, complete author effort and all repair episodes.
