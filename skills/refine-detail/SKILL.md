# Refine Detail

Develop one human-owned white paper linked from the current product description. Accept the paper at its supplied location and preserve that location; there is no standard input folder. Keep immutable source snapshots, research handoffs, and revision ledgers under `product/<product>/`. Generated `documents/<product>/<doc-name>/` folders are output only. Do not create a second editable paper or impose a fixed heading template.

Resolve the product and selected paper from the user's request. Load repository instructions and the current product model. The paper must have a `White paper:` declaration classified as a `reference` source claim. If the link was newly attached and the model has not been refreshed, route that source-only interpretation through `refine-design` before consuming it; do not classify the link as a capability or make paper prose into PRD requirements. Run the installed refinement helper `node ../refine-design/scripts/white-paper-references.mjs --current <product/current.json> --repo <repository>` to bind exact source bytes and check the current link. Work on the one selected paper.

Before an agent edit, read and preserve the paper's current bytes. Inventory every distinct fact, constraint, claim, decision and rationale, alternative, question, uncertainty, dependency, experiment/result, and citation. Give the fresh read-only `technical-researcher` one bounded question set and this current inventory, paper, accepted product/technical context, repository facts, and still-current evidence. Ask it for sourced findings, evidence limits, alternatives, consultation requests, and a proposed revision. Include authored diagrams in that review: check their participants, arrows, timing/ownership claims and surrounding qualifications against the current proposal. Preserve relevant diagrams with their explanatory context for technical preparation; correct misleading diagrams rather than treating them as decoration. Consult the system architect only for a material system boundary and the Polylith architect only for a material feature/service/registry question. Reuse current assessments when their inputs are unchanged.

The parent opens material primary sources, distinguishes research from accepted decisions, resolves owner-visible product choices with the owner when needed, and edits the authored paper. It may reformat, reorder, merge repetition, rewrite prose, and add findings, but every inventoried item must remain intelligible or be explicitly resolved or superseded with its reason and history. Contradictions and unanswered questions remain visible. New claims carry source and decision status. Do not let the research agent edit files or certify its own proposal. Format and check the final paper with the working repository's installed Prettier and resolved configuration before calculating its hash, verifying the revision ledger, or saving research bound to those bytes. If the repository declares Prettier but it is not installed locally, report that blocker rather than using a global formatter.

After editing, prepare a revision ledger mapping every prior inventory item to a quoted before excerpt and a quoted after excerpt, with `retained`, `resolved`, or `superseded` disposition and a reason for the latter two. Use `node scripts/revision-ledger.mjs --before <exact-before-copy> --after <paper> --ledger <ledger.json> --product-root <product/<product>>` to verify the excerpts and save the ledger. This mechanical check does not prove semantic equivalence; the parent must check the meaning and restore any unexplained omission. If the owner directly edits the paper, treat the new source as authority rather than restoring content they deliberately removed.

Save useful research evidence under `product/<product>/white-papers/research/<sha256>.json` as a digest-named, immutable `technical-paper-research` JSON report with `schemaVersion: "1.0"`, repository-relative `paper`, and the exact `paperSha256`. Include the actual research, source URLs/versions, status, and unresolved questions. Technical context identifies whether the saved report matches current paper bytes; a stale report is evidence of past analysis, not current authority. Hand technical preparation a concise inventory of paper claims and open questions with suggested existing-record matches; it must classify unresolved questions through the same product, technical, and evidence gap records as questions from the primary description. Report changed decisions, source limits, and whether existing technical context or guide pages have become stale. Technical preparation and `generate-technical` own the general guide; do not hand-edit its receipt-owned pages. Do not implement application code through this skill.
