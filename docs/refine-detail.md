# Refine a technical white paper

[`refine-detail`](../skills/refine-detail/SKILL.md) develops one authored technical white paper linked from a product description. It is hosted in this repository and installed globally with the other managed skills. Use [attach-detail](attach-detail.md) first to declare the existing paper; then ask Codex to research and refine a specific issue in it.

The skill checks the link against the current product model and binds the paper's exact bytes. A fresh read-only `technical-researcher` examines a bounded question, primary sources, alternatives, and uncertainty. The parent checks material evidence, routes product or architectural choices to their owners, and edits the single authored paper at its supplied input location.

The paper can be rewritten and reorganized. Before editing, the parent inventories its facts, constraints, decisions, rationale, alternatives, questions, experiments, and citations. After editing, each item must remain intelligible or be explicitly resolved or superseded with its history. The final paper is formatted and checked with the working repository's local Prettier before its digest and revision ledger are saved. A revision ledger records exact before-and-after excerpts under `product/<product>/`; the mechanical check confirms coverage entries, while the parent judges whether meaning was preserved. Research reports and immutable source snapshots are support data, not duplicate editable papers.

The skill does not publish a PRD or general technical guide. A saved research handoff binds the paper's exact digest; technical context marks it current or stale when the paper changes. Technical preparation maps the paper's substantive claims into ordinary technical records and routes its open questions through the same gap categories as questions from the primary description. The generated guide gives the paper a focused mapping section as well as links to those shared decisions and questions. See the [white-paper workflow](../planning/implementation-agents/technical-white-paper-workflow.md) for the authority and change-impact contract.
